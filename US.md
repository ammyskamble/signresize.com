## 1. Executive Summary & Market Opportunity

The United States represents a high-intent, high-value digital utility market with millions of annual document and photo submissions across **Federal Immigration (USCIS, DS-160)**, **Standardized & Board Exams (College Board, LSAC, USMLE, Bar)**, **Federal & Remote Employment (USAJOBS, Form I-9)**, and **Everyday Life (State DMV REAL ID, 4x6 Pharmacy Passport Prints)**.

### The Unfair Advantage of SignResize in the US
* **100% In-Browser Privacy (HTML5 Canvas & WASM):** Unlike commercial competitors that upload private face biometrics and identity cards to third-party cloud servers (triggering CCPA, FERPA, and HIPAA concerns), SignResize processes all files locally on the user's machine. Zero server uploads. Zero data retention.
* **No Paywalls or Watermarks:** Competitors charge $7–$19.99 or slap watermarks on cropped images. SignResize offers an immediate, modern, friction-free experience.
* **The Pharmacy Print Hack ($0.35 vs. $19.99):** CVS and Walgreens charge up to $21.99 for two 2x2 passport photos. Our 4x6 sheet generator lets users order a standard $0.35 photo print with 6 passport cuts.
