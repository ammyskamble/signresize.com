Who are eligible for ‘Free Travel Authority’ (free Railway Pass)?
उत्तर केिल अनुसूवचत जावत (SC) और अनुसूवचत जनजावत (ST) के उम्मीदिार ही ' वनःशुल्क यात्रा िावधकरण ' (वनःशुल्क रेलिे पास) का लाि उठा
सकते हैं।
Candidates belonging to Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) only can avail ‘Free Travel Authority’ (free
Railway Pass).

This facility is available only to SC/ST candidates. While filling up ONLINE application, such candidates, should

reservation. They are also required to upload their Caste Certificate.
