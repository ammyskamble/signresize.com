Information Brochure pertaining to Recruitment of
Enrolled Persons in Indian Coast Guard
1. Indian Coast Guard is an Armed Force of the Union under Ministry of Defence.
ICG is a multi - mission force, conducting round-the-year operations for the protection
of our national maritime boundaries. The recruitment of Enrolled Persons in the ICG
is conducted through Coast Guard Enrolled Persons Test (CGEPT) to following posts:
-
1.1. Navik (General Duty)
1.2. Navik (Domestic Branch)
1.3. Hygienist-I
1.4. Yantrik (Electrical)
1.5. Yantrik (Mechanical)
1.6. Yantrik (Electronics)
2. The current recruitment procedure is divided into various stages and elaborate
activities take place in each stage. The brief outline of each stage is enumerated in
the succeeding paragraphs.
2.1. Stage-I Computer Based Examination at various cities across the
country.
2.2. Stage-II Stage-II is conducted at designated ICG premises only.
Currently, there are nine recruitment centres which are Gandhinagar, Chennai,
Visakhapatnam, Goa, Kochi, Mangalore, Kolkata, Bhubaneswar and Noida. It
is only qualifying in nature and comprises following major activities which are
common for all irrespective of cadre or caste category of candidates: -
2.2.1. A Basic Identity Check.
2.2.2. Physical Fitness Test (PFT). A physical fitness test with
standards mentioned at para 6 below.
2.2.3. Basic written examination/ Assessment & Adaptability Test.
2.2.4. Document Verification
2.2.5. Recruitment Medical Examination.
2.3. Stage-III (Induction and Enrolment process) The procedures
conducted during Stage-III at INS Chilka are mentioned below.
2.3.1. Aadhar based verification
2.3.2. Biometric Verification of candidates
2.3.3. Document Verification of all original documents

2.3.4. Pre-Enrolment Medical. Candidate are examined for final
medicals.
2.4. Enrolment. On completion of all the above formalities the candidates
are enrolled into ICG and their training commence at INS Chilka.
3. Eligibility Conditions. MALE and FEMALE INDIAN CITIZENS possessing
educational qualifications and age as prescribed below are eligible to apply.
3.1. Post Wise Educational Qualification.
3.1.1. Navik (General Duty). Class 12th passed with Maths and
Physics from an education board recognized by Council of Boards for
School Education (COBSE).
3.1.2. Navik (Domestic Branch). Class 10th passed from an
education board recognized by Council of Boards for School Education
(COBSE).
3.1.3. Hygienist-I. Matriculation OR Industrial Training Institute (ITI)
OR equivalent from Board of Education recognised by the Central/
State Government.
3.1.4. Yantrik. Class 10th passed from an education board recognized
by Council of Boards for School Education (COBSE) AND Diploma in
Electrical/ Mechanical / Electronics/ Telecommunication (Radio/Power)
Engineering of duration 03 or 04 years approved by All India Council of
Technical Education (AICTE).
OR

Council of Boards for School Education (COBSE) AND Diploma in
Electrical/ Mechanical / Electronics/ Telecommunication (Radio/Power)
Engineering of duration 02 or 03 years approved by All India Council
of Technical Education (AICTE).

5. Tattoo Policy The policy regarding acceptability of permanent body tattoos of
candidates aspiring to join the Indian Coast Guard is amplified in the succeeding
paragraphs: -
5.1. Location of Tattoo(s). Permanent body tattoos are only permitted on
inner face of forearms i.e. from inside of elbow to the wrist and on the reverse
side of palm/ back (dorsal) side of hand. Permanent body tattoo on any other
part of the body is not acceptable and candidate will be barred from
recruitment.
5.2. Size of Tattoo(s). Only small innocuous tattoos that are not prejudicial
to good order and military discipline are permitted e.g. religious symbols or
names of near and dear ones.

--- PDF PAGE 18 ---
5.3. Nature of Tattoo(s). A tattoo must not have any lewd or offensive
indelible marks or figure on any part of the body. Tattoos that are indecent
(grossly offensive to modesty, decency, or propriety), sexist (demean a person
based on gender) or racist (degrade or demean a person based on race
ethnicity or region and religion).
5.4. Exception of Tattoo(s) on Candidates From Tribal Communities.
Candidates from tribal communities/ from tribal areas as declared by the
Government of India Scheduled Tribes Orders/ Act/ Lists (amended and
modified from time to time), are permitted to have permanent body tattoos on
any part of the body as per existing customs and traditions of the said tribe to
which a candidate belongs.

8. Job profile The Enrolled Persons in ICG are the operational backbone of the
service. They are responsible for sustaining sea-going operations, maintaining
complex naval systems, coordinating logistics support, and safeguarding national
maritime boundaries. The post wise details of day-to-day functions pertaining to
various posts are as follows: -
8.1. Navik(GD): - Navik (General Duty) serves onboard ships and ashore for
various duties, as an Enrolled Personnel. Navik (GD) candidates are allotted
specialization trade on completion of basic training at INS Chilka. The trade/
specialization (i.e. Quarter Arms, Radar Plotter, Radio Operator, Mechanical
Engineer, Electrical, Store, Writer) is allotted on the performance of the
candidate in the training and the requirement of the service. The trades include.
8.2. Navik(DB): - The Navik (Domestic Branch) provide logistics support to
the Ships Company onboard ship and ashore as Cook & Steward as mentioned
below:
8.2.1. Cook: The would be required to prepare food as per menu (both
vegetarian and non/vegetarian including handling of meat products) and
accounting of ration. In addition, they will also be allotted other duties for
efficient running of the organisation.
8.2.2. Steward: They would be required to serve food in the Officer’s
mess as waiters, housekeeping, maintenance and accounting of funds,
wine and store handling, preparation of menu etc. In addition, they will
also be allotted other duties for efficient running of the organisation.
8.3. Hygienist-I: - Hygienist-I post is of Sweeper/Safaiwala and duties
includes maintaining hygiene, Cleaning of WCs of toilets onboard ICG
ship/shore, maintenance of septic tank/sewer lines/Bathrooms onboard Indian
Coast Guard Ships, Shore establishment premises. Also, the post is
transferrable anywhere in India (mainland/islands) subject to the exigencies of
the service. Any other duties as and when assigned by the concerned Officials.
8.4. Yantriks: - Yantriks serves on-board ships and aircraft as per the
branch allocated to them {Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics} at INS Chilka.
They are responsible to maintain all types of machinery/equipment fitted on-
board Ships/ Aircraft/ Station. They are also required to maintain the adequate
level of spares and accounting of spares. In addition, they will also be allotted
other duties for efficient running of the organisation.
