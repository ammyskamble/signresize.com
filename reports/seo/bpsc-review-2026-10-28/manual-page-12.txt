STEP 6: PHOTO AND SIGNATURE
 Section is about live photo and Signature.
 While capturing the live photo, the candidate should ensure that he/she keeps his/her face straight and in front of the
Webcam and looks into the Webcam with open eyes. Use good lighting and light coloured background. Do not wear
any headgear such as cap or power glasses, sunglasses, mask, muffler, helmet etc. Make sure that no other person is
in the frame, and the photo is clear, with your face fully visible.Next Part the applicant needs to upload a picture of
Hindi and English Signature with set of instruction like the picture size should be less than 20 kb, width should be
between 150 to 220 PPX and height should be between 250 and 320 PPX.

editing/updating, the profile has to be locked.
