Documents Requirements

X Standard (SSC) Marksheet/Certificate is required.

  • Please pay attention to upload only a good quality recent photograph. JAM 2027 Admit Card and Scorecard will be printed with the photograph that you submit during application.
  • The photograph must match with your appearance on the day of the examination.
  • A passport size (3.5 cm Width × 4.5 cm Height) photograph of the face of the candidate is required for the application form.
  • The photograph must be recent and must be in COLOUR. Photographs of poor resolution taken using a mobile phone and other self-composed portraits may result in the rejection of the application.
  • The photograph is required in JPEG/JPG format and must be of size 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm (Width × Height).
    Maximum size of the JPEG/JPG image can be 480×640 in pixels (0.3 Mega pixel).
  • The minimum pixel size of the JPEG/JPG image should be 240×320 in pixels. The file size of the image should be in the range of 50 kB to 200 kB. Background of the photograph must be white or a very light colour.
  • The face should occupy at least 50% of the area of the photograph with a full-face view looking into the camera directly.
  • The main features of the face must not be covered by hair of the head, any cloth/facemask or any shadow. Forehead, eyes, nose and chin should be clearly visible.
  • If glare cannot be avoided then take the photo without the spectacles.
  • You must not wear spectacles with dark or tinted glasses, only clear glasses are permitted. Poor quality of the photograph submitted will lead to rejection of your JAM application, without any refund of the application fee.
