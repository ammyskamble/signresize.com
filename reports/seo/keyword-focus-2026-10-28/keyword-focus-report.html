Where to limit new effort

  • Broad “signature resize”: keep improving the homepage, but its position 63.96 makes it a longer-term target. The homepage’s all-query position 10.44 does not mean this exact keyword ranks at 10.
  • Police physical tests, syllabus, salary and eligibility: retain useful existing guides, but do not let high impressions drive the main tool roadmap. For example, “1600 meter police bharti time” has 162 impressions and zero clicks at 7.88. This is informational demand; tool usage and revenue are unknown.
  • Ambiguous and brand-like queries: do not build pages for “ha” or “yes”. Treat “sign resize” as ambiguous and “signature resize.in” as potentially another brand’s navigation. Neither establishes strong performance for “signature resize”.
  • International expansion: India accounts for 1,006 of 1,012 clicks. Maintain existing international pages but prioritize India in the next optimization cycle.
  • Year-specific variants: verify application cycles and official sources before updating year labels. Do not create duplicate pages for every spelling, date or punctuation variant.
