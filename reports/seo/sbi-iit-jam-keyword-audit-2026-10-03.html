Small fixes, in order

Separate JAM from GATE

Live GATE/JAM header: 480 × 160 px, 5 × 2 cm, 5–200 KB. Its tool: 400 × 160 px. Official JAM 2027 guidance: 7 cm width × 2 cm height, minimum 280 × 80 px to maximum 560 × 160 px when expressed width first, JPG/JPEG, 50–150 KB.

Fix: Use a separate JAM data entry and preset. Remove JAM applicability from GATE content and shared navigation labels; retain a link to the JAM tool. Reverify GATE separately before altering its settings.

Show the correct year and source

The pages claim “Official 2026 Guidelines” without an external official source link in their main content. The JAM 2026 brochure gives 10–150 KB; JAM 2027's current documents page gives 50–150 KB.

Fix: Clearly label the exam cycle, source and actual verification date. If supporting older applicants, provide explicitly labeled separate year settings. Avoid universal rules across exam cycles.

Correct SBI unit conversions and claims

SBI pages display 140 × 60 px as 4 × 2 cm, and the template describes these measurements as based on 200 DPI. At 200 DPI, 140 × 60 px converts to about 1.78 × 0.76 cm. A reviewed official SBI notification specifies 10–20 KB and prefers 140 × 60 pixels, black ink on white paper, and no capital-letter signature. This example is a specialist recruitment notification; check each current PO/Clerk notification before claiming those cycles are verified.

Fix: Lead with pixels and KB. Remove unsupported centimetre equivalence, compulsory cursive/ballpoint claims, bank-record matching requirements, and guarantees of portal acceptance unless the exact official notification supports them.

Give each SBI page a clear purpose

Broad SBI intent is distributed between the combined IBPS/SBI page and PO/Clerk pages. The main navigation points to a combined banking destination.

Fix: Target the broad term on the SBI hub and IBPS terms on the IBPS page. Keep PO/Clerk pages if they offer useful recruitment-specific differences; cross-link them naturally. If they are substantially identical, choose one destination and permanently redirect redundant pages after reviewing Search Console. Overlapping targeting alone does not prove competing pages are suppressing each other's rankings.

Make the result easy to verify

The shared tool advertises several upload modes and a drawing feature. Exam applicants need a clear path from their handwritten scan to a correctly sized JPG.

Fix: Lead with upload, crop and download. Show actual output width, height, format and bytes/KB; verify both minimum and maximum file sizes. Make typed/drawn signature options clearly separate from exam handwritten-scan guidance. Test portrait and landscape phone photos, tiny inputs, and downloads on mobile.
