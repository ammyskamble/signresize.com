# Layout change audit

Comparison: current workspace against the last committed version. Some changes were already present in the workspace before the border-design work.

## Hero issue corrected

- The homepage hero had been moved into a closed “How the resizer works” disclosure, below the tool and preparation sections.
- A short text introduction had replaced the visible hero and its main heading.
- Compact styling disabled the hero card's glow animation.
- Restored the original hero content and animation above the studio, with one main heading. Kept its compact dimensions.

## Other current changes

- Signature, Photo and Documents are tabs within the studio instead of separate large cards.
- Main panels use thin borders, smaller corners, less shadow and less padding.
- Empty upload and output canvases are shorter; loaded images retain working space.
- Desktop dimension/KB fields share a row; narrower export panels use two columns.
- The repeated preset summary and desktop step guide are hidden. The preset selector and official-rule notice remain visible.
- The extra quick-upload strip is hidden; Browse File, drag/drop, Try Sample and Draw Signature remain in the canvas.
- Knowledge-base converter and quick rules were moved below the guides to eliminate the tall empty column.
- Article, troubleshooting, author and footer spacing was reduced on resizer pages.
- Source image/background notices appear in Image Enhancement. Export checks open at download.
- Custom exports validate against active settings and warn when they differ from the chosen exam preset.
- File intake rejects empty, oversized and unsupported files. Upload text now describes image input; PDF remains an export option.
- Upload/processing sequence guards prevent older asynchronous results from replacing newer files/results.
- Hindi and Marathi tabs keep translated labels. Dedicated presets and name/date/document controls are retained.
- Local preview routes are excluded from the sitemap.

These layout changes have not been deployed to the live site in this chat.
