🏛️ Pillar 1: Portal, Examination Cycles & Online Registration

1. ctet (Central Teacher Eligibility Test)

National Benchmark
❓ WHAT is CTET?

The ==Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) is a national qualifying examination== administered by CBSE under Section 23(1) of the Right to Education Act. It certifies minimum eligibility for teaching appointments in Classes 1 to 8 across Indian central and state institutions.

⚠️ WHY is CTET Mandatory?

Without a CTET qualifying certificate, candidates are ==statutorily disqualified from applying for regular PRT and TGT posts== in Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS), Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS), DSSSB Delhi schools, and Army Public Schools.

⏰ WHEN is CTET Conducted?

CBSE administers the examination ==twice annually in July and December cycles==. The qualifying certificate holds ==lifetime validity== from the date of result declaration.

🛠️ HOW to Qualify CTET?

Score ==60% or higher (90 out of 150 marks)== for General candidates, or ==55% (82 out of 150 marks)== for SC, ST, and OBC candidates across 150 multiple choice questions with zero negative marking.

2. ctet nic in (Official Gateway)

Official Portal
❓ WHAT is ctet nic in?

The ==sole official online gateway hosted by National Informatics Centre (NIC)== for all CTET operations: online registration, information bulletins, image discrepancy resolution, admit cards, and provisional answer keys.

⚠️ WHY do Portal Errors Occur?

Server traffic surges during the closing days of the application window cause ==session timeouts and payment gateway gateway verification delays==. Beware of fraudulent duplicate domains mimicking the official portal.

⏰ WHEN to Access the Portal?

Access the portal ==during non-peak hours (early morning before 8:00 AM or after 10:00 PM)== to complete fee payments or resolve image discrepancy flags without gateway drops.

🛠️ HOW to Safely Use ctet nic in?

Always confirm the browser address bar shows ==https://ctet.nic.in== with an authentic SSL security certificate. Never enter registration credentials on third-party recruitment blogs.

3. ctet 2026 (Current Recruitment Blueprint)

Active Cycle
❓ WHAT is ctet 2026?

The ==current national examination series administered by CBSE== covering offline pen-paper OMR tests across more than 135 designated examination cities in India.

⚠️ WHY are 2026 Guidelines Crucial?

The 2026 cycle strictly incorporates the ==Supreme Court B.Ed restriction ruling== and enhanced machine-learning image discrepancy scanning during form submission.

⏰ WHEN are ctet 2026 Key Dates?

Online registrations remain active for ==approximately 25 to 30 calendar days==, followed by a 4-day correction window, with offline examination scheduled on Sundays.

🛠️ HOW to Register for ctet 2026?

Navigate to the active application link on ctet.nic.in, complete 4-step registration, upload compliant photo/signature, and download the Confirmation Page.

4. ctet notification (Official Bulletin)

Legal Bulletin
❓ WHAT is the ctet notification?

The ==comprehensive Information Bulletin released in PDF format by CBSE== outlining legal eligibility, language codes, fee schedules, exam shifts, and document specifications.

⚠️ WHY must Candidates Read the Notification?

Selecting an improper minimum qualification code or mismatched language medium during registration leads to ==invalidation of examination results during physical scrutiny==.

⏰ WHEN is the Notification Released?

Published ==roughly 60 to 75 days before the scheduled exam date== on the public notices dashboard of ctet.nic.in.

🛠️ HOW to Track Notification Updates?

Download the official PDF bulletin from ctet.nic.in, check Annexures for examination city code lists, and review Section 5 for document byte size restrictions.

5. ctet online form (Registration Portal)

Web Module
❓ WHAT is the ctet online form?

The ==digital registration application form== where aspirants enter personal identification, academic credentials, paper selection, language combinations, and test city preferences.

⚠️ WHY do Submissions Fail?

Forms fail due to ==oversized image files exceeding 100 KB==, special characters in address fields, or payment gateway debits failing to reconcile with CBSE servers.

⏰ WHEN is the Submission Deadline?

The online form window ==closes strictly at 11:59 PM on the final specified date==; late submissions or physical postal applications are not accepted.

🛠️ HOW to Fill the Form Without Errors?

Pre-compress your photo to 10–100 KB and signature to 4–30 KB before logging in. Keep Class 10 certificate handy to enter name and parentage with identical spelling.

6. ctet application form (Candidate Dossier)

Candidature Record
❓ WHAT is the ctet application form?

The ==legal record of candidature== containing candidate photograph, scanned signature, qualification codes, reservation claims, and fee transaction identifiers.

⚠️ WHY is Verification Critical?

CBSE checks the application form against candidate identity at exam centers. ==Discrepancies in name, date of birth, or photo mismatch cause center barring==.

⏰ WHEN can Details be Edited?

Corrections can be made ==only during the official 3 to 5 day CBSE Correction Window==; no modifications are permitted once the window expires.

🛠️ HOW to Preserve Application Records?

Download and print at least ==two copies of the official Confirmation Page== immediately after successful payment; this is mandatory during recruitment document verification.

7. ctet login (Dashboard Authentication)

Security Portal
❓ WHAT is ctet login?

The ==secure authentication portal requiring Application Number and Password== that gives candidates access to their personalized dashboard on ctet.nic.in.

⚠️ WHY do Login Locked Errors Occur?

Entering an incorrect password 3 consecutive times or typing case-sensitive security PIN captchas incorrectly causes ==temporary session lockout==.

⏰ WHEN is Login Required?

Login is required ==at 5 lifecycle stages==: initial form completion, correction window editing, city slip download, admit card download, and result scorecard access.

🛠️ HOW to Recover Forgotten Credentials?

Click "Forgot Password" or "Forgot Application No." on the login portal. Verify identity using the ==registered mobile OTP or security question option== to reset credentials.

🧠 Pillar 2: Eligibility Criteria, Syllabus, PYQs & Passing Scores

8. ctet eligibility (Statutory Qualifications)

NCTE Standards
❓ WHAT is ctet eligibility?

The academic criteria set by NCTE: ==Paper 1 requires Senior Secondary (50%) + 2-year D.El.Ed / BTC==; ==Paper 2 requires Graduation (50%) + B.Ed or 2-year D.El.Ed==.

⚠️ WHY are B.Ed Candidates Barred from Paper 1?

By judgment of the Supreme Court of India dated August 11, 2023, ==B.Ed qualifications do not meet primary pedagogical standards==; B.Ed holders can only apply for Paper 2 (Classes 6 to 8).

⏰ WHEN can Appearing Students Apply?

Candidates ==pursuing any teacher training course in any semester or year== are legally eligible to register under Supreme Court directives.

🛠️ HOW to Confirm Eligibility Code?

Check Section 3 of the Information Bulletin. Select Education Qualification Code matching your degree and passing/appearing status accurately.

9. ctet syllabus (Curriculum Blueprint)

Pedagogy Matrix
❓ WHAT is the ctet syllabus?

The official 150-mark curriculum: ==Child Development & Pedagogy (30 marks)==, Language 1 (30), Language 2 (30), and Domain Subjects (60 marks: EVS/Maths for Paper 1; Maths & Science or Social Studies for Paper 2).

⚠️ WHY do Candidates Fail Pedagogy?

Pedagogy constitutes ==90 out of 150 total marks across all sections==. Memorizing static facts without understanding constructivist child-centered teaching causes score failure.

⏰ WHEN does the Syllabus Change?

The core syllabus adheres to ==NCERT curriculum guidelines and NEP 2020 frameworks==; major structural revisions are announced months in advance via gazette notices.

🛠️ HOW to Complete the Syllabus Efficiently?

Master theories of ==Piaget, Vygotsky, and Kohlberg== first. Read NCERT textbooks (Classes 3–5 for EVS; Classes 6–8 for Maths/Science/Social Studies) line-by-line.

10. ctet passing marks (Qualifying Thresholds)

Score Benchmarks
❓ WHAT are ctet passing marks?

The prescribed benchmark: ==60% (90 out of 150 marks)== for General (UR) category, and ==55% (82 out of 150 marks)== for SC, ST, OBC (NCL), and PwD candidates.

⚠️ WHY is 82 Marks Accepted for 55%?

55% of 150 equals 82.5 marks. CBSE and recruiting bodies legally recognize ==82 marks as qualifying for reserved categories== after fractional rounding benefits.

⏰ WHEN are Passing Marks Applicable?

Qualifying status is applied ==permanently upon result generation==; there is no expiration date or normalization deduction after scoring.

🛠️ HOW to Guarantee Reaching Passing Marks?

Because CTET has ==zero negative marking==, answer every single question on the OMR sheet. Aim for 25+ in CDP and 20+ in each language to safely cross 100+ aggregate marks.

11. ctet cut off (Eligibility vs Hiring)

Benchmark Standard
❓ WHAT is the ctet cut off?

CTET does not operate on a percentile or variable cut-off. It enforces a ==static qualifying threshold: 90 marks for General and 82 marks for Reserved categories==.

⚠️ WHY do Recruitment Cut-Offs Differ?

CTET confirms eligibility; employer bodies like ==KVS, NVS, and DSSSB conduct subsequent competitive hiring exams== with separate competitive cut-off merits.

⏰ WHEN does a Higher Score Help?

State recruitment commissions (e.g. Super TET in UP or BPSC TRE in Bihar) may ==allocate weightage points based on CTET percentage== during academic merit compilation.

🛠️ HOW to Improve Your CTET Score?

There is ==no ceiling on attempt limits==. Candidates who have already qualified can re-register in subsequent cycles to improve their marks without forfeiting previous certificates.

12. ctet previous year question paper (Practice Papers)

Revision Asset
❓ WHAT are CTET PYQs?

The ==authentic examination question papers with official final answer keys== released by CBSE after each offline session from 2011 to 2026.

⚠️ WHY are PYQs Essential?

Over ==40% of pedagogy scenarios and EVS environmental questions repeat== conceptually. Solving PYQs trains candidates to eliminate common distractor options.

⏰ WHEN to Start Solving PYQs?

Begin solving subject-wise PYQs after reading NCERT theory, and ==dedicate the final 20 days exclusively to full-length timed mock tests==.

🛠️ HOW to Practice PYQs for Maximum Speed?

Print out official CBSE OMR sheets. Practice bubbling answers in a strict ==150-minute timed environment== to master time management and avoid misaligned bubbling.

📸 Pillar 3: Photo, Signature Specifications & Image Discrepancy Removal

13. ctet photo resize (Passport Image Tool)

Biometric Standard
❓ WHAT are CTET Photo Dimensions?

The mandatory specification: ==3.5 cm width × 4.5 cm height (280×360 px at 200 DPI)== with a file weight strictly bounded between ==10.0 KB and 100.0 KB in JPG format==.

⚠️ WHY do Upload Portals Reject Photos?

Uploading files below 10 KB triggers blur warnings; files above 100 KB fail server uploads. ==Selfies, caps, tinted spectacles, or patterned backgrounds trigger automatic disqualification==.

⏰ WHEN must Photos be Re-uploaded?

Re-upload is required ==within 48 to 72 hours of receiving an SMS alert== notifying you of an image discrepancy during the application window.

🛠️ HOW to Resize Photo for CTET?

Use the client-side CTET Photo Resizer. It locks 3.5×4.5 cm bounds, ensures 80% face focus against white background, and compresses to compliant JPG size in seconds.

14. ctet signature size (Signature Image Tool)

Script Bounds
❓ WHAT is the ctet signature size?

Official bounds: ==3.5 cm width × 1.5 cm height (140×60 px at 200 DPI)== with a file weight strictly between ==4.0 KB and 30.0 KB in JPG format==.

⚠️ WHY are Signatures Disqualified?

Writing your name in ==CAPITAL / BLOCK LETTERS triggers automated cancellation== under CBSE rules. Lined notebook paper or faint blue ink also trigger scrutiny rejections.

⏰ WHEN to Prepare Scanned Signatures?

Sign and scan on unruled white paper with black ballpoint ink ==prior to initiating online form registration== to avoid portal session expirations.

🛠️ HOW to Convert Signature to 4–30 KB?

Remove notebook shadows with Make Background White, then use the CTET Signature Tool to crop to 3.5×1.5 cm and lock file size under 30 KB.

15. ctet image discrepancy (Upload Error Resolution)

Error Clearance
❓ WHAT is a ctet image discrepancy?

An ==automated scrutiny flag placed on your candidature== indicating that your uploaded photograph or signature does not comply with CBSE clarity, contrast, or dimension criteria.

⚠️ WHY must Discrepancies be Cleared?

Unresolved image discrepancy flags ==automatically withhold admit card generation==. You will not be permitted to enter the examination center.

⏰ WHEN is Status Updated?

Once a compliant image is re-submitted, the CBSE administrative scrutiny desk reviews the file and updates the portal status to ==Admin Approved within 48 to 72 hours==.

🛠️ HOW to Remove Image Discrepancy?

Log in to ctet.nic.in -> click ==Remove Image Discrepancy== -> upload fresh 10–100 KB photo or 4–30 KB signature -> authenticate OTP -> verify Admin Approved status.

16. ctet correction window (Modification Protocol)

Editing Facility
❓ WHAT is the ctet correction window?

The ==official 3 to 5 day modification window== opened on ctet.nic.in allowing candidates to edit permitted fields in their submitted application form without forfeiting application fees.

⚠️ WHY are Some Fields Locked?

Registered email address and mobile numbers cannot be altered for security reasons. Permitted edits include ==Name spelling, Father/Mother name, Category, Paper opted, and Exam City==.

⏰ WHEN does the Correction Window Open?

Typically opens ==5 to 7 days after the final date of online form submission==; dates are announced via a dedicated public notice on ctet.nic.in.

🛠️ HOW to Edit Details During Correction Window?

Log in with Application Number and Password -> click ==Edit Application Details== -> make modifications -> authenticate via mobile OTP -> download revised Confirmation Page.

📜 Pillar 4: Hall Tickets, Answer Keys, Results & DigiLocker Issuance

17. ctet admit card (Hall Ticket)

Examination Entry Pass
❓ WHAT is the ctet admit card?

The mandatory hall ticket specifying ==exact center address, roll number, shift timing, reporting hours, and candidate photograph and signature==.

⚠️ WHY are Admit Cards Withheld?

Admit cards are withheld for candidates with ==unresolved image discrepancies, fee payment reconciliation failures, or duplicate registrations==.

⏰ WHEN is the Admit Card Released?

CBSE releases an advance ==City Intimation Slip 10 days before the exam==, and the ==Final Admit Card strictly 2 days prior to the exam date==.

🛠️ HOW to Download and Print?

Log in at ctet.nic.in with Application Number and DOB -> download PDF -> print on clean A4 paper -> carry along with original government photo ID and ballpoint pen.

18. ctet answer key (Provisional & Final Keys)

OMR Evaluation
❓ WHAT is the ctet answer key?

The master key containing official correct options alongside the ==scanned copy of candidate OMR answer sheets== for all paper codes.

⚠️ WHY do Key Challenges Matter?

Challenges allow subject experts to rectify erroneous question formulations or answer choices. ==Accepted challenges result in bonus marks awarded to all candidates==.

⏰ WHEN is the Key Published?

Provisional keys are uploaded ==15 to 20 days after the offline exam session==; the online challenge window stays active for 3 to 4 days.

🛠️ HOW to Challenge an Answer Key Question?

Log in to ctet.nic.in -> select question number and paper code -> upload NCERT documentary proof -> pay ==₹1,000 per question challenge fee (refundable if accepted)==.

19. ctet result (Scorecard Declaration)

Official Outcome
❓ WHAT is the ctet result?

The official declaration of marks obtained out of 150 along with ==explicit qualification status for Paper 1 and Paper 2==.

⚠️ WHY is Result Verification Needed?

Recruitment boards like KVS and State PSCs verify CTET score validity directly against CBSE databases to prevent fake qualification claims.

⏰ WHEN is the Result Announced?

CBSE declares results within ==25 to 35 days of the examination date== on both ctet.nic.in and cbseresults.nic.in.

🛠️ HOW to Check Your Score?

Access the direct result link on ctet.nic.in -> enter Roll Number -> view subject marks -> print scorecard for immediate record.

20. ctet certificate download (DigiLocker Digital Credential)

Digital Credential
❓ WHAT is the CTET Certificate?

The ==digitally signed, QR-code encrypted eligibility certificate and marksheet== issued exclusively via DigiLocker under the Information Technology Act 2000.

⚠️ WHY are Physical Certificates Not Mailed?

CBSE discontinued paper certificate printing to eliminate postal losses, reduce paper waste, and allow instant cryptographic verification by hiring commissions.

⏰ WHEN is the Certificate Available?

Uploaded to DigiLocker ==approximately 20 to 30 days after the result declaration date== and remains valid for a lifetime.

🛠️ HOW to Download from DigiLocker?

Download the DigiLocker app or visit digilocker.gov.in -> log in with Aadhaar-linked phone -> search ==Central Board of Secondary Education== -> select ==Teacher Eligibility Test Certificate== -> input Roll Number and passing year -> download digitally signed PDF.
