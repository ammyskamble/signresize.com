❓ Frequently Asked Questions (IIT Kharagpur · JAM 2027)

What is the IIT JAM 2027 signature size and dimension? ▼

The official JAM 2027 document guidelines require a scanned signature in JPG/JPEG format, file size strictly between 50 KB and 150 KB, signed inside a 7 cm wide × 2 cm high rectangular box (pixel limits: 280×80 px minimum, 560×160 px maximum).

Why does JOAPS reject signatures below 50 KB? ▼

JOAPS enforces a strict 50 KB lower bound to ensure signatures retain crisp visual detail when scaled on Admit Cards and Scorecards. Clean digital scans often compress to 10–20 KB; SignResize automatically applies lossless JPEG comment padding to securely lift the file into the 50–150 KB corridor.

Can I use capital letters for IIT JAM signature? ▼

No. Signatures written in ALL CAPITAL LETTERS or using only initials are disqualified by the organizing IIT without refund. Sign naturally in running handwriting.

Is the IIT JAM signature size the same as GATE? ▼

No. IIT JAM requires a 7×2 cm box (560×160 px) and 50–150 KB. GATE specifies 480×160 px and 3–300 KB. Use the dedicated tools for each respective exam.

What pen color should I use for IIT JAM signature? ▼

Use a black or dark blue ink ballpoint or gel pen on clean, unruled white paper. Do not use pencils, sketch pens, or lined notebook paper.
