What is the mandatory file naming rule on the Join Indian Coast Guard portal? ▼ The portal requires strict file names: Signature.jpg for signature, Passport_Photograph.jpg for chalkboard slate photo, and Thumb_Impression.jpg for left thumb impression. What is the chalkboard slate photograph requirement for Coast Guard? ▼ Candidates must take a passport-size photo holding a black chalkboard/slate in front of their chest with their Full Name and Date of Photo capture written clearly in white chalk.
