COAST GUARD ENROLLED PERSONS TEST (CGEPT) –BATCHES 01/2027 & 02/2027

1. Eligibility Conditions. Online applications are invited from MALE and FEMALE INDIAN CITIZENS
possessing educational qualifications and age as prescribed below, for recruitment as an Enrolled Person in the
Indian Coast Guard through Staff Selection Commission.
2. Post Wise Educational Qualification.
2.1. Navik (General Duty). Class 12th passed with Maths and Physics from an education board
recognized by Council of Boards for School Education (COBSE).
2.2. Navik (Domestic Branch). Class 10th passed from an education board recognized by Council of
Boards for School Education (COBSE).
2.3. Hygienist-I Matriculation OR Industrial Training Institute (ITI) OR equivalent from Board of
Education recognised by the Central/ State Government.
2.4. Yantrik. Class 10th passed from an education board recognized by Council of Boards for School
Education (COBSE) AND Diploma in Electrical/ Mechanical / Electronics/ Telecommunication
(Radio/Power) Engineering of duration 03 or 04 years approved by All India Council of Technical
Education (AICTE).
OR
Class 10th & Class 12th passed from an education board recognized by Council of Boards for School
Education (COBSE) AND Diploma in Electrical/ Mechanical / Electronics/ Telecommunication
(Radio/Power) Engineering of duration 02 or 03 years approved by All India Council of Technical
Education (AICTE).

Selection Procedure
5. The selection of candidate is based on their performance in Stage-I, II, III & IV (explained in detail in
subsequent paras), meeting the laid down medical standards during medical examination and the number of
vacancies available for the post in respective batch. Qualification in Stage-I, II, III, IV is compulsory for
recruitment in ICG. The selection of candidates for the post of Yantriks will be only for CGEPT-01/27 and for
Nvk(DB) & Hygienist-I will for CGEPT-02/27. However, selection of candidates for the post of Navik(GD) in
either CGEPT-01/27 or CGEPT-02/27 batch will be based on order of merit, available vacancies and
administrative requirement. Candidates cannot claim their selection in a particular batch. All candidates will
be compulsorily subjected to identity check prior commencement of the examination of Stage- I, II & III of CGEPT.
The identity check will include verification/ matching of following at all stages of selection procedure: -
5.1. Live Image Capture during Registration – The candidate has to upload the latest photograph
during registration. Additionally, live image of the candidate will be captured during the registration. The
facial features of the candidate in the uploaded photo will be matched against the Real-time photo.
Moreover, the real time photo of the candidate captured during the registration of online application form
and the photograph of candidate in application form will be matched with facial features/ physical
appearance of the candidate in all subsequent Stages.
5.2. Biometric
5.2.1. Only left thumb biometric will be captured at the Stage-I recruitment before the online exam.
In case the biometric of left thumb is not captured during Stage-I then the biometric of right thumb
will be captured and will be used for subsequent verification. Apart from left and right thumb, no
other finger will be considered for the biometric.
5.2.2. It is the candidates’ responsibility to check beforehand that the biometric machines are able
to capture the fingerprint image. In case, if the biometric machine is not able to capture the
fingerprint image due to Mehndi, wax, etc. then, the candidate will not be allowed to appear in the
examination.
5.2.3. Signature as uploaded in online application.
5.2.4. Identification mark as mentioned in online application.
Note: Failure in even one of the above-mentioned identity check at any stage will lead to cancellation of
candidature.

documents for verification while reporting at their respective examination centre: -

download E-admit card, candidates are required to upload additional documents as mentioned in
para 7.5. and select preferred date & centre within a given period. Failure to upload additional

OMR based examination to qualify for further stages of Stage-II recruitment process. This test is
qualifying in nature and the marks obtained will not be included in final merit list. The result will
be announced at the venue after the conduct of examination.
6.2.4. Document Verification. All information provided in online application has to match with
all original documents produced by the candidate like Class 10th /Class 12th / Diploma marksheet
Identity proof (Aadhaar Card OR PAN Card OR Voter ID card OR Driving License OR Passport)/
category certificate/ individual subject and aggregate marks/CGPA (as applicable)/Domicile
Certificate. The candidates will be declared either Pass or Fail in document verification. Any
mismatch/ inconsistency/ error with information provided by the candidate in application form,
uploaded document and original documents produced for verification at Stage-II with the
documents in respect to “Name, Date of birth, Parent’s name, subject wise marks/ CGPA (as
applicable), validity of documents, category certificate details, Domicile Certificate etc.” will lead
to rejection in document verification. The date of issue of all documents has to be on or before
the closing date of application. The validity of all uploaded documents has to be at least up to
31 Dec 27. Any false declaration in the online application will lead to cancellation of candidature.
Document Verification shall be conducted at multiple stages. Any mismatch, inconsistency,
discrepancy or submission of false/ fabricated documents at any stage shall render the candidate
liable for cancellation of candidature including after enrolment, in accordance with applicable
rules.
6.2.5. Recruitment Medical Examination. The Recruitment Medical Officer will assess the
candidates for medical fitness as per laid down medical standards and declare them fit or unfit.

All the candidates who have qualified document verification will be subjected to
recruitment medical examination. Candidates who are found medically unfit in Recruitment
Medical Examination and who wish to appeal will be immediately referred to the designated

(allocated by ICG) are the final authorities on declaring a candidate fit or unfit during
recruitment medical examination, appeal medical examination and medical examination
prior to enrolment.
6.2.6. Preparation of Final Merit List for Stage-III. ICG may carry out the re-verification (in
the absence of candidates) of documents submitted by candidates and only those candidates will
be considered for merit preparation who will finally qualify the document verification and declared
fit in recruitment/ appeal medicals. Merit list {Zone wise & category wise) for Nvk(GD) MALE and
all India (category wise) for Nvk(GD) FEMALE, Yantriks, Navik(DB) & Hygienist-I will be prepared
for candidates who have qualified all the Stages and meeting the requisite criteria as mentioned
in this advertisement. Shortlisting of candidates for the post of Navik(GD) in either CGEPT-01/27
or CGEPT-02/27 batch will be based on order of merit and available vacancies. Candidates
cannot claim their selection in a particular batch. The candidates’ order of merit will only be based
on marks scored in Stage-I, qualification in all phases of Stage-II and number of available
vacancies. Assessment test marks shall not form part of the final merit but shall be used only for
breaking a tie. The tie-Breaking Rule in Merit, in case of two or more candidates having the same
marks in Stage-I, is as follows (wherever tie is broken next stage will not be preceded): -
6.2.6.1. Candidate older in age will be higher in merit.
6.2.6.2. Candidate scoring more marks in Assessment Test.
Note: - Candidates applying under SC/ ST and desirous to have their fare reimbursed are to bring original

cancelled cheque leaf for NEFT payment and travel form downloaded from website for claiming the Travel
Allowance during Stage-II. No request for claiming the travel allowance will be entertained post

eligible for enrolment.

documents submitted during Stage-III with the concerned Boards/ Universities/ State Government
authorities. Candidate will be terminated from service if documents are reported as not genuine by
respective Boards/Universities/State government.

13. Medical Examination and Standards.
13.1. Medical examination will be conducted by authorized Medical Officers of Defence Forces as
per medical standard prescribed in current regulations applicable to Enrolled Persons on entry.
13.2. Mandatory Medical Standards
13.2.1. Height Minimum height standard for male candidates is 157 cms and for female
candidates is 152 cms. Height standards may be reduced by up to 05 cms for candidates having
domicile of Assam, Nagaland, Mizoram, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Tripura,
Garhwal, Kumaon and Sikkim. Reduction of 05 cm is also applicable to Gorkhas. Height
standards may be reduced by upto 02 cms for candidates having domicile of Ladakh,
Lakshadweep & Minicoy Islands and Andaman & Nicobar Islands.
13.2.2. Chest Chest wall should be well proportioned and well developed. Minimum chest
circumference for male candidate should be 77 cm with chest expansion of atleast 05 cm. For
female candidates the chest should be well proportioned with minimum expansion of 05 cm.
13.2.3. Weight Proportionate to height and age as applicable for ICG.
13.2.4. Hearing. The candidate should have normal hearing i.e. able to hear focused
whisper from a distance of 06 m by each ear separately.
13.2.5. Tattoo Permanent body tattoos are not permitted on any part of the body.
However, certain concessions are permitted to candidates belonging to tribal areas communities
as declared by the Govt. of India. For other candidates, permanent body tattoos are only permitted
on inner face of forearms i.e. from inside of elbow to the wrist and on reverse side of palm/back
(dorsal) side of hand.
13.2.6. Dental Should have healthy gums, good set of teeth and minimum 14 dental
points.
13.2.7. General Health Candidate should be fit for the Armed forces, must conform to the
medical standards. Candidate should be of normal anatomy without loss or deformity of any
appendages. He/ She should be free from any active or latent, acute or chronic, medical or
surgical disability or communicable disease, infection and skin ailments. Candidate shall be
physically and mentally FIT to perform duty in any part of the world, in any climate and terrain
places.
13.2.8. Gender Any candidate if found to have predominant characteristics of the opposite
gender as evident on external physical examination, will be rejected as unfit. Any candidate
having undergone gender reassignment surgery will be declared Unfit.
13.2.9. Pregnancy (for female candidates only) Any candidate, if found to be pregnant shall
be disqualified and her candidature will be rejected.
13.2.10. Gynaecological Examination (for female candidates only) The examination
covers external genitalia, hernia orifices and the perineum. Any evidence of stress urinary
incontinence or genital prolapse outside introitus shall lead to disqualification of the candidate.
